ABOUT ME

Turning Spatial Data Into Practical GIS Solutions

I am a GIS Analyst with more than three years of experience in spatial data management, transportation and infrastructure mapping, cartography, geodatabase maintenance, quality assurance, and spatial analysis. My professional experience includes creating and maintaining GIS datasets, validating spatial and attribute information, integrating GPS and imagery, and producing analytical and thematic mapping products using ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, and ArcGIS Online.

My academic and project work has expanded my experience into network analysis, environmental GIS, remote sensing, emergency-response planning, raster analysis, and geospatial automation. I enjoy taking complex spatial information and converting it into clear, useful outputs that support planning, infrastructure management, public safety, and evidence-based decision-making.

WHAT I BRING

What I Bring to a GIS Team

I bring a combination of hands-on GIS experience, strong spatial-data fundamentals, and a practical problem-solving mindset. I have experience maintaining geospatial datasets, performing topology and attribute QA/QC, integrating GPS and imagery sources, conducting buffer, overlay, proximity, and network analyses, and producing clear cartographic products for technical and non-technical audiences.

I am comfortable working independently as well as collaborating with multidisciplinary teams, and I place strong emphasis on data accuracy, documentation, consistency, and delivering reliable GIS outputs on schedule.

PROFESSIONAL FOCUS

My Professional Focus

My goal is to use GIS to make spatial information easier to understand, analyze, and apply to real-world problems. I am particularly interested in infrastructure and municipal GIS, transportation, environmental analysis, emergency planning, spatial data management, and geospatial automation.

I am continuously developing my technical skills so I can contribute to more efficient GIS workflows and stronger data-driven decision-making.
