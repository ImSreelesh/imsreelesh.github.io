Automated Forest Regeneration Priority Analysis

ArcGIS Pro • Python • ArcPy • Jupyter Notebook • Spatial Automation

Project Overview

This project automated a forest-regeneration priority workflow for Adelaide Metcalfe, Ontario, using Python and ArcPy in ArcGIS Pro. The analysis identified high-priority areas that could maximize forest interior and medium-priority areas located within 100 metres of existing natural-heritage features.

Municipal boundaries, natural-heritage areas, roads, and hydrology datasets were extracted, clipped, buffered, and processed through a scripted workflow. Areas located within 30 metres of roads were excluded from the final regeneration-priority zones.

Project Objectives

  • Automate the forest-regeneration workflow using Python and ArcPy.
  • Extract the Adelaide Metcalfe municipal boundary.
  • Clip natural heritage, roads, and hydrology datasets.
  • Identify high- and medium-priority regeneration areas.
  • Remove priority zones located within 30 metres of roads.
  • Calculate final regeneration areas in hectares.

Methods and Tools

The Python environment was configured in ArcGIS Pro with ArcPy, workspace paths, geodatabases, input data, and output locations. Municipal and environmental datasets were then selected and clipped to the study area.

A 100-metre buffer was generated around natural-heritage features. Centroids and calculated-radius circular buffers were created to represent potential forest-interior expansion areas. Difference, merge, road buffering, erase, and dissolve operations were used to generate the final priority dataset.

Skills Demonstrated

Python scripting, ArcPy geoprocessing, automated workspace management, field calculations, geometry calculations, clipping, buffering, centroid generation, difference analysis, merging, erasing, dissolving, environmental suitability analysis, and cartographic design.

Project Outcome

The automated workflow produced high- and medium-priority forest regeneration zones for Adelaide Metcalfe. The final output included calculated regeneration totals and a professional map showing the relationship between existing natural heritage and potential woodland-expansion areas.
