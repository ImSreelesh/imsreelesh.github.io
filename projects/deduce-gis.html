Professional Overview

From July 2020 to November 2023, I worked as a GIS Analyst with Deduce Technologies in Bangalore, India. My work supported transportation, infrastructure, logistics, land-use, building, and point-of-interest mapping projects across India, the United States, the United Kingdom, and Europe.

I created, maintained, and validated high-accuracy spatial datasets using ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, JOSM, satellite imagery, aerial imagery, GPS information, and field-survey data. My work included feature digitization, attribute editing, topology validation, coordinate-system management, geodatabase maintenance, and cartographic production.

I also collaborated with multidisciplinary technical teams to resolve GIS issues, document workflows, meet client specifications, and deliver mapping products within established timelines and quality requirements.
